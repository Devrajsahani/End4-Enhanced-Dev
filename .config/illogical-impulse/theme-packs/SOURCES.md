## Rights and attribution

- Wallpaper images belong to their respective authors and repositories. Local copies are for personal desktop use; check each upstream repository and individual image rights before sharing or redistributing.
- Some mixed wallpaper collections do not provide a license covering every image, and the car source repository has no repository-level license file. Treat these copies as personal desktop use and do not redistribute them without checking the original authors’ rights.
- The Tokyo Night wallpaper repository and Nordic Wallpapers repository publish repository-level MIT licensing; that does not necessarily override third-party rights for individual images.
